# Content guidelines

Every practice, declaration, routine or habit template added to the app must follow these rules.

## 1. No medical claims

- Healing content is prayer and declaration, never medical advice.
- Drop pseudo-science (e.g. "700 trillion volts in your cells").
- Healing screens carry: _"This does not replace medical care. Keep seeing your doctor."_
- Reason: App Store / Play Store reject unverified health claims, and people must not skip treatment.

## 2. Respect copyright

- Sermons and transcripts belong to their teachers. Summarize in our own words, attribute, link the source.
- Short quotes only. Longer quotes need written permission.
- Bible text: public-domain translations only (BSB, WEB, KJV). NIV/ESV/NKJV need a license.

## 3. Label the teacher

- Content packs live in `src/content/packs/<teacher>/` with `teacher`, `source` and `version`.
- Views specific to one teacher or stream (free will, tongues, healing) are shown as that teacher's, not as the app's doctrine.

## 4. Grace, not condemnation

- Missed days and slips are data for adjusting the system, not reasons for shame.
- Wording points back to identity in Christ.

## 5. Review

- A pastor or trusted leader reviews prayer and spiritual-warfare content before public release.
